i'm making this post because i want to list the software i use and like, with descriptions on some of them, so that whoever reads this learns new things maybe and also because i was bored


- [7zip](https://www.7-zip.org/) absolutely incredible archiver for windows. can basically open any filetype, works on anything and is super light and fast. 

- [firefox](https://www.firefox.com/) it's alright, theres some areas for improvement but its great for regular web browsing and ad blocking works well

- [vs code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) primary ide, i use it for my projects writing notes and stuff. not exactly a great piece of software but the one i'm most comfortable with

- [fl studio](https://www.image-line.com/) amazing DAW. no words needed its just that good

- [krita](https://krita.org/) image editing

- [vlc](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/) i mean come on

- [obs studio](https://obsproject.com/) i mean come on x2


- [everything voidtools](https://www.voidtools.com/) so so good for finding things because i can't keep my files organized ever. absolute must have

- [godot engine](https://godotengine.org/) very good game engine with a very good editor definitely worth a shot


- [kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/) for light and quick video editing. loads fast and is easy to use for quick edits
- [davinci resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) for anything else, completely free and stupidly overkill

- [msys2](https://www.msys2.org/) if you're doing anything with C/C++ on windows this is a must have. you get everything *nix but native windows and everything links with windows


- [mediakit](https://github.com/samxyz30/mediakit) super good for quick video trimming or audio trimming/conversion
- [ffmpeg](https://www.ffmpeg.org/) same thing but cli


- [wikipedia](https://wikipedia.org/) not exactly software but you know i just had to put it in here


theres more, i might update this list later some day.
